About this preview

NutriGo is in development, not yet a store release. The screens above are real Android emulator captures with a synthetic account. Widget images are native Android test renders; physical Home and Lock Screen checks are still pending.

Production sign-in, physical-device health sync, push delivery, and wider restaurant coverage still need release validation. Food search uses USDA, and published recipes come from MedlinePlus.
