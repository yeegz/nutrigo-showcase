Delete your account.

You can request deletion here, even if you no longer have the app.

Email a deletion request

Or write to ygshuh@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my NutriGo account”.

What to include

Provide your NutriGo handle or sign-in email. We’ll verify ownership before deleting the account and reply when the request is complete. If you use Apple’s Hide My Email, include the relay address associated with NutriGo if you know it. Do not send your password, verification codes, or identity documents.

Prefer to do it in the app?

Open Settings → Account → Delete account. Confirm your sign-in and the deletion. Deactivation is a separate option and keeps your records.

What is removed

Your NutriGo sign-in account, profile, food and water logs, measurements, saved meals, recipes, reminders, uploaded photos, posts, and social connections are removed from the account service. Failed cleanup is retried; deletion is confirmed only after that process finishes.

Previously viewed copies and screenshots held by other people cannot be recalled. Offline devices clear account data when they reconnect. Manage records retained in Apple Health or Health Connect in that service; deleting NutriGo does not erase your entire health store.

What may remain

An opaque revoked-device marker remains to prevent old devices from replaying actions; it contains no account ID or health profile. Service security logs and the email correspondence used to verify your request are handled separately, as described in our privacy policy.

NutriGo is developed by Yousof Selim. You do not need to reinstall the app to submit this request.
